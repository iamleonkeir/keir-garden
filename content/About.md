## Who I am?

I'm Leon Keir. People call me Keir ... or Leon. I answer to both. It doesn't matter why. Though if you stick around you might find out.

I have a messy, wonderfully non-linear brain. It makes connections easily, and forgets where it put them just as readily. Most of what I do is an effort to work with that rather than against it: Attempting to keep things tidy on the outside so the inside is free to digress.

I'm drawn to diverse subjects. From training for functional strength to esoteric teachings, from building things myself to the strange new business of working alongside AI. On paper these look like totally separate hobbies. In my mind they're one long conversation, and this digital garden is where I'm mapping that out. At least in part.

My name from a previous life. If you knew me then: hello 👋.

## Why a digital garden?

Because my notes were never going to be a neat shelf of finished essays. A garden suits a mind like mine. Things get planted half-formed, linked to whatever they remind me of, and tended when I come back to them. Some grow. Some don't. That's fine.

It also keeps me honest. Writing in the open, even messily, turns scattered thoughts into something I can find again, and something you can wander through. [[How this garden grows]] explains how to read the place.
