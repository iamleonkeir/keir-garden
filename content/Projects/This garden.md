## How it's built

- **Written** in [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md), as plain notes in a folder
- **Turned into a website** by [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz), which keeps the links, the graph and the search
- **Kept** on [GitHub](https://github.com/iamleonkeir/keir-garden), in the open
- **Served** by Cloudflare, which rebuilds the site every time I publish

I write a note, publish, and a minute later it's live.

## Why it's built this way

It's free, it's fast, and it's mine. The notes are plain text files that will outlive any of these tools. Nothing is locked into a platform.

It was also built with [[AI and agents|Dude]]: I chose the shape and wrote the words that matter; Dude did the setup and the checking. See [[Thinking in notes]] for why a garden suits me.
