## About the course

Awakening Your Light Body is the foundation course of Orin and DaBen's light body work, channelled by Sanaya Roman and Duane Packer. It's six parts of guided audio journeys, each with written lessons, that gently open and strengthen your energy.

I share it as a sponsor. The original words and recordings are theirs and stay as they made them; what I've added is a home for them online. The course comes in three modules of two parts each, with time between them for practice and integration, and everything a learner needs is in one place: the journeys, the lessons, and a quiet record of how far you've come. It's self-paced, but it gently guides rather than dictates.

## Who it's for

Anyone drawn to the work. It's the starting point, so no previous experience is needed, only the willingness to listen regularly and notice what happens.

## How to join

The course is by invitation. Once you're invited, sign in with your email address: you'll get a one-time code, so there's no password to remember.
